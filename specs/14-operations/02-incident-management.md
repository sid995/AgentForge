# Incident Management

## Severity

- SEV1: broad outage, security breach, or uncontrolled production impact.
- SEV2: major degraded workflow or tenant impact.
- SEV3: limited degradation with workaround.

## Process

1. Declare and assign incident commander.
2. Establish communication channel and timeline.
3. Mitigate impact before deep root-cause work.
4. Preserve evidence and changes.
5. Verify recovery through user-facing signals.
6. Produce blameless postmortem and tracked actions.

## Postmortem fields

Summary, impact, detection, timeline, root cause, contributing factors, mitigation, what worked, what failed, corrective actions, owners, due dates, and recurrence tests.
